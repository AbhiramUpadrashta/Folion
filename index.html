Everything you reach for in a PDF editor.
Without the heavy app.

Edit existing text

Tap a word on the page and type. Folion matches the font, size and color, and really removes the old words when you save.

Photos become editable

Snap a page with your camera or open a scan. Built-in text recognition turns it into lines you can change, in 17 languages.

Sign & fill

Draw, type or upload your signature and reuse it. Fill PDF forms, tick boxes, add dates and stamps.

Mark it up

Highlight, draw, add shapes and arrows, sticky-note comments, images, watermarks and page numbers.

Organize pages

Merge PDFs and images, drag to reorder, rotate, duplicate, delete, extract or split into separate files.

Real redaction

Black out private details. Redacted pages are flattened on save, so the hidden text can't be copied back out.

Find & replace

Change a name or a word across the whole document in one go, keeping the original look.

Save your way

Export a PDF with real text, a smaller file for email, or JPG, PNG and WebP images. Share straight to Drive or Files on phones.

Questions

Is Folion really free?

Yes. No trial, no watermark, no account. It's one of the small apps from Bubbles Aro.

Can I change text in a photo or a scanned page?

Yes. Choose Edit text → Make text editable. Folion reads the words on the page, then you tap a line to change it. Clear, well-lit photos work best.

Do my files get uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your device. The only download is the text-reading engine the first time you use it.

How do I save to Google Drive?

On a phone, tap Export → Share / Save to Drive and pick Drive. On a computer, download the file and drop it into Drive.

Can I install it like an app?

Yes. In Chrome or Edge use Install app from the address bar; on iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen. It then opens full screen and works offline.

Which fonts does it keep?

Folion picks the closest match (sans, serif or mono, bold and italic) and keeps the size and color. Text in standard fonts stays real, searchable text in the PDF.
